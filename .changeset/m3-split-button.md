Add `SplitButton`: the M3 Expressive split button, matching Compose. It has a leading action and a trailing button that opens a `Menu`, in five sizes and the filled, tonal, outlined and elevated styles. Inner corners press-morph per size; while the menu is open the trailing button turns round, its chevron turns over, and its icon stays optically centred. `buttonVariantClasses` exposes Button's variant colours.
