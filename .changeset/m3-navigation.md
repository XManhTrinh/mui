Add `NavigationRail` / `NavigationRailItem` and `NavigationBar` / `NavigationBarItem`: the M3 Expressive navigation rail (collapsed, expanded and modal expanded) and the flexible navigation bar, matching Compose. The rail springs open to fit its widest item; the modal rail opens as a sheet over a scrim. Items are links or buttons marked with `aria-current`, with a pill that grows when current.
