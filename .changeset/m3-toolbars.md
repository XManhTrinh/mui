Add `DockedToolbar`, `FloatingToolbar`, `ToolbarFab` and `useToolbarScrollExpansion`: M3 Expressive toolbars matching Compose. The docked toolbar is a full-width 64px bar of actions. The floating toolbar is a horizontal or vertical pill in standard or vibrant colours whose leading and trailing content collapses; paired with a FAB, collapsing hides the toolbar while the FAB grows from 56px to 80px. Arrow keys move between controls (mirrored in RTL), and keyboard focus expands a collapsed toolbar. `useToolbarScrollExpansion` collapses the toolbar while content scrolls down.
