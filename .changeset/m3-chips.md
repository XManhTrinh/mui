Add `AssistChip`, `SuggestionChip`, `FilterChip` and `InputChip`: M3 chips matching Compose, flat or elevated. Their padding follows Compose's arrangement for each icon combination. Filter and input chips morph from 12px corners to round when selected (Compose's Expressive shapes), and filter chips grow a check. Input chips pair the chip's action with a remove button, which Backspace or Delete also triggers.
