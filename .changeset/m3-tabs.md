Add `Tabs` and `Tab`: M3 primary and secondary tabs matching Compose, built on React Aria. Tabs can have icons above or before their labels, scroll when there are too many, and drive the selected tab's panel. An indicator slides to the selection on the default spatial spring, and in scrollable rows the selection scrolls to the centre. Arrow keys follow the laid-out text direction.
