Add `Badge`, `BadgedBox` and `Divider`. Badges are Compose's 6px dot and 16px label badge in `error` colours; `BadgedBox` places one at the top end of an icon without positioning anything or changing the icon's size, mirrored in RTL. Dividers are 1px `outline-variant` lines, horizontal or vertical, optionally inset 16px at the start or both ends.
