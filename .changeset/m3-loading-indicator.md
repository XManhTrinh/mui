Add `LoadingIndicator`: the M3 Expressive loading indicator, matching Compose. Indeterminate, it morphs through seven Material shapes on a spring while rotating; determinate (`value` 0–1), it morphs from a circle to a soft burst. It has `default` and `contained` variants, custom `shapes`, and progressbar semantics; under reduced motion it only rotates. The shape library gains `cubicsToPath` and path translation.
