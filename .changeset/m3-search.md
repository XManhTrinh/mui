Add `SearchBar` and `SearchAppBar`: the M3 Expressive search bar, a 56px pill that expands into a search view, either a docked dropdown over a scrim or a full-screen view that grows from the bar, for suggestions or results. Pressing the bar, typing into it or ↓ expands it; ↓ moves into the results, Enter submits, Escape or a press outside collapses it. `SearchAppBar` is Compose's app bar with search, with pinned and enter-always scrolling. `usePresence` (primitives) takes the transitioned property that ends an exit.
