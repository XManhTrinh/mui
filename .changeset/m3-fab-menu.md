Add `FabMenu` and `FabMenuItem`: the M3 Expressive FAB menu, matching Compose. The FAB (default, medium or large) shrinks into the 56px close button while the items appear from the button upwards on Compose's stagger, each springing out to its width. It has primary, secondary and tertiary colour sets, start, centre and end alignment, `aria-expanded`, and arrow-key navigation, and closes on Escape, an outside press or choosing an item.
