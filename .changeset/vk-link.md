Add `Link` to `@vkieu/mui/vk`: a text link that looks and behaves the same everywhere. `variant` is `inline` (underlined, for links in running text) or `standalone` (underlined on hover, focus and press); `tone` is `primary` or `inherit`; `size` inherits the text or sets a label role. `external` (or `target="_blank"`) opens a new tab with `rel="noopener noreferrer"`, an "open in new" icon and `labels.newTab` for screen readers. Routed through `RouterProvider`, with the M3 focus indicator. Tokens: `--vk-link-color`, `--vk-link-underline-thickness`, `--vk-link-underline-offset`.
