Add `TooltipTrigger` / `Tooltip` and `RichTooltipTrigger` / `RichTooltip`: M3 plain and rich tooltips matching Compose. Plain tooltips show on hover or keyboard focus, describe their trigger, stay while hovered and close on Escape. Rich tooltips are non-modal popovers with a subhead, text and an action, opened by press. Both scale in from their anchor and can draw a caret.
